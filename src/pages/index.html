The system

Three forms of capital

Social×Intellectual×Financial

Most networks hold one form of capital and hope the others follow. SIF compounds all three inside one structure.

Social capital

Named introductions and verified relationships. Every member enters through someone who vouches for them.

Intellectual capital

Domain Expert Councils turn scattered knowledge into reviews that hold up under scrutiny.

Financial capital

Members who invest decide for themselves, with expert analysis in hand and the network behind it.

How it works

From introduction to decision.

Every opportunity follows the same route. Nothing reaches members without a named source and an expert review.

  1. Member-led sourcing

    Opportunities come from members who know the company and stand behind the introduction.

  2. Expert review

    A Domain Expert Council examines the market, the technology and the team.

  3. Member briefing

    Reviewed opportunities are shared with certified members inside the private group.

  4. Individual decision

    Each member decides independently. SIF is the intermediary, not the investor.

  5. Recognised contribution

    Members who source opportunities and make introductions are recognised under the membership terms.
