How to join

Entry is named.

Every member joins through an introduction from someone already in the network.

  1. Introduction

    A current member introduces you, or you write to us and name who referred you.

  2. Questionnaire

    You tell us about your experience and what you bring to the network.

  3. Certification

    Before you see any opportunity, you self-certify as a high-net-worth individual or a sophisticated investor.

  4. Conversation

    A short call with the team, then a decision on your membership.

Questions

Before you apply.

Does SIF invest my money?

No. SIF is an intermediary. It brings opportunities to members, and each member decides independently whether to take part. SIF does not manage money or give investment advice.

Who can see specific opportunities?

Only members who have self-certified as high-net-worth individuals or sophisticated investors, using the forms set out by the Financial Conduct Authority. Certifications are renewed every 12 months. Opportunity details are shared only inside the private member group and must not be passed on.

Can I upgrade from Club to Founding?

Yes, at any time. The price difference is prorated.

What does the fee pay for?

Your place in the network: events, research, expert reviews and the community. The membership fee is not an investment and does not entitle you to any return.

What are the risks of private market investing?

Private market investments are high risk. They can be hard to sell, their value can change sharply, and you can lose all the capital you invest. Nothing on this site is an offer or a recommendation.
