Opportunities

Every opportunity is reviewedbefore members see it.

SIF brings opportunities to its members. Domain Expert Councils review each one, and members decide for themselves.

Review process

Four stages before a briefing.

  1. Sourcing

    Opportunities come from founders, VCs and industry contacts in the network.

  2. Screening

    The team reviews the business model, the market and the founding team.

  3. Expert Council review

    Domain experts examine the technology, market dynamics, competition and financial plans.

  4. Member briefing

    Opportunities that pass are shared with certified members, together with the experts' analysis.

Asset areas

Where the network looks.

Real world assets

Real estate and other tangible assets, with a focus on yield and long-term value.

Secondaries

Positions in later-stage and pre-IPO companies.

Tech ventures

Pre-seed and seed companies and corporate spin-offs.

For members

How members take part.

Specific opportunities are shared only inside the private member group, with members who have self-certified as high-net-worth or sophisticated investors.

  1. Briefing

    Certified members receive a briefing by email and on the member platform.

  2. Review

    Members read the materials and expert reports, and can join a Q&A with the founders where one is held.

  3. Decision

    Each member decides independently. Where a member takes part, regulated custodians and partner law firms handle payments and legal documents.

  4. Updates

    Members who take part receive company updates and reports from the company over time.

Understand the risk.

Private market investments are high risk. They can be hard to sell, their value can change sharply, and you can lose all the capital you invest. An expert review reduces some risks. It does not remove them.

Nothing on this page is an offer, an invitation to invest or investment advice.
